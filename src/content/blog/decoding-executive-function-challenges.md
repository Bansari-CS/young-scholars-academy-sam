Executive function is the brain's management system. Many gifted and neurodivergent learners have strong ideas but weak execution.

## What it looks like at home

- Trouble starting simple tasks
- Forgetting multi-step instructions
- Big emotions during transitions

## What helps most

Reduce hidden steps, externalize routines with checklists, and celebrate completed process actions, not just perfect outcomes.
