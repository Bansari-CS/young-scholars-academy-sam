A low-arousal space helps sensitive learners stay regulated and available for learning.

## Reduce sensory noise

Start with lighting, sound, and visual clutter. Small environmental shifts can reduce overwhelm quickly.

## Support sustained focus

Offer predictable routines, sensory tools, and gentle transitions to help children maintain attention with less stress.
