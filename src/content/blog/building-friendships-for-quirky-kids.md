Parallel play can be a powerful social bridge for neurodivergent teens.

## Why proximity matters

For many learners, connection happens first through shared space and shared activity, not immediate conversation.

## Build gradual social confidence

Create low-pressure environments where teens can participate side-by-side and opt into deeper interaction over time.
