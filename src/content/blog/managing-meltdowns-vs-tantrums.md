Meltdowns are nervous-system overload. Tantrums are goal-directed behavior. The response should differ.

## During overload

Lower demands, reduce sensory input, and focus on safety and co-regulation.

## After recovery

Debrief gently: identify triggers, update supports, and practice replacement routines when calm.
