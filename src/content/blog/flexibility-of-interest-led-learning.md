Interest-led learning is not "anything goes." It is structured freedom.

## Start from a passion anchor

Choose one topic your child is already obsessed with, then map reading, writing, math, and projects into that theme.

## Keep outcomes visible

Track weekly skills, not just activity volume. This keeps learning intentional while preserving motivation.
